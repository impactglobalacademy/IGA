Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a programme?

Visit our Programmes page, choose the programme that interests you, and click "Enrol Now". You'll need to create an account or log in to complete the enrollment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept bank transfers, credit cards, and mobile payments. Contact our programmes team for specific payment arrangements and installment options.

Do you offer corporate training?

Yes! We offer customized programmes for organizations. Contact our partnerships team to discuss your specific needs and create a tailored solution.

Can I get a refund?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with the programme. Refunds after this period are handled on a case-by-case basis.

Do you offer scholarships?

We offer limited scholarships for exceptional candidates. Please reach out to discuss your situation and see what options might be available.
